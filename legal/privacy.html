Life Hub privacy policy

Life Hub is a personal app made and used by one person for their own everyday life. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What bank data is used

When the owner connects their own bank account through Enable Banking, Life Hub reads the transactions of that account: date, amount, the name of the shop or person, and the description. Balances may be read too.

Why

Only to show the owner their own spending, income, budgets and savings inside Life Hub.

Where it is stored

  • On the owner’s own phone (browser storage).
  • In the owner’s own private cloud storage (Firebase) and private GitHub repository, used to fetch new transactions automatically.

The data is never sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

How long

As long as the owner keeps it. The owner can delete everything at any time in Life Hub, and can end bank access at any time in their bank app or by letting the consent expire.

Contact

Questions about this app: contact the owner through the GitHub account that hosts Life Hub.
